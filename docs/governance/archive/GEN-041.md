# GEN-041 — N3: the notices acknowledgement surface (cut B)

**ACTS ONLY.** The first resident-authored writes in the notices domain. No
routes, no read surface, no contract change.

- **D1** `markRead` and `acknowledge` are once-only; `respond` is append-only with
  **current = latest**, because a resident may change their answer and the earlier
  one is the record of what they said before.
- **D2** the **audience snapshot** is the authorization — not a grant, and not
  where the resident lives today. The first place GEN-037's snapshot does
  load-bearing work.
- **D3** responses are a small declared list on the assertion; no declared
  question means `respond` is refused. Free text waits on ADR-019 OQ4.
- **D5** accepted while `published` or **expired** — expiry ends visibility, not
  accountability — and refused on draft, pending, approved and withdrawn.
- **D6** **measured, not chosen:** a resident act writes NO `access.audit_event`
  row and cannot, because `principal_id` references `access.staff_principal`. The
  provenance lives in the domain table, exactly as `home_safety.acknowledgement`
  already does.
- **D7/D8** declared stances per act, and an explicit no-reason-code entry so the
  checks see a stance rather than an omission.
