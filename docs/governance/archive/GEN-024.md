# GEN-024 — W1a: the authority table takes the assertion model

`GEN-023` measured that **revoking a grant was not expressible by the runtime at all**: shortening
`effective_to` is an `UPDATE` that `pemo_app` cannot issue, and appending a closing row was refused
by `scope_grant_no_overlapping_periods`, because the original grant's *asserted* period still ran to
infinity.

Shape **A′** was ruled: `scope_grant` becomes an ADR-013 assertion series, the exclusion constraint
is dropped with a stated rationale, and its hygiene role passes to a write-path advisory-lock guard
plus a whole-table detector in the battery. The constraint was measured to be hygiene rather than
safety — rights are a union and monotonic, so a duplicate live grant authorizes nothing new.

**What this buys is the ability to revoke authority at all.**
