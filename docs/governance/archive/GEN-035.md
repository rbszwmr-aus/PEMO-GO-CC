# GEN-035 — ADR-018 hardening: authority owns its precondition

`GEN-033` held one gap out at close: **every `G-2` site received a pre-expanded node set and could not
verify it had been filtered to `grant_administrator` grants.** The three W3 routes filter correctly;
a fourth passing the expansion of *all* the caller's grants would have widened every floor to *any
grant anywhere*, with nothing to catch it.

This makes the wrong input **unrepresentable** rather than merely discouraged. Behaviour-preserving.
