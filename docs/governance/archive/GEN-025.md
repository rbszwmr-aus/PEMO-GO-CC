# GEN-025 — W1b + W1c: the platform spine

**One PID, two independent oracles, reported separately.**

**W1b** — `access.audit_event` gains `source`, makes the three authority columns nullable, and binds
them with one biconditional CHECK, so `ADR-010` `A1`'s provisioning act can be audited honestly
instead of with a fabricated principal. Plus `reason_code` (`ADR-014`) and the validation mechanism.

**W1c** — `platform.idempotency_record` is replaced by the `ADR-015` shape. `GEN-023` measured the
shipped one running the act **twice** under concurrency, keyed without a principal and storing a
response blob. The replacement is written by the audited-write primitive in the same transaction as
act and audit — **the second shared-primitive touch since `GEN-011`**.

**The named trap:** a provisioning act has no principal, so `principal_id` is NULL in the uniqueness
tuple, and Postgres treats NULLs as distinct by default.
