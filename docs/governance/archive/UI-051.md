# UI-051 — Notices write flow (management-web) against frozen 2.1.0

## What to build

Part 2 of the notices staff surface, over the contract `GEN-047` froze at `2.1.0` with
Amendment 1 applied: every read now carries `permittedActions` (the contract's own act
operationIds, allowed-only, on the object AND on the container `ScopeTreeNode`),
`stateEnteredAt`, and both title languages on the list. `UI-045` built the reads and
rendered no control because the client could not say who may press; `2.1.0` is the
server saying so, and this PID renders exactly what it says.

- **D0 Carry-ins.** The untracked UI readouts in the part-1 worktree (grant-admin
  readout, local try-out guide) land in this PID's first commit, the way an ADR is
  carried by the PID that first cites it.
- **D1 Affordances are read, never reasoned.** A control renders iff its operationId
  is in `permittedActions` of the notice (`reviseNotice`, `submitNotice`,
  `approveNotice`, `declineNotice`, `publishNotice`, `correctNotice`,
  `withdrawNotice`) or of the scope node (`composeNotice`). No visibility decision
  reads `lifecycleState`, a role, or any literal. Guarded by a source scan, and by
  two mutations under the `UI-045` R1 criterion: a control hard-coded visible reds
  the named test; visibility derived from the state field reds on the lifecycle
  fixture, where the two answers differ (a draft the caller may publish; a
  pending notice the caller may not approve).
- **D2 Compose.** Entry from a scope-tree node whose `permittedActions` lists
  `composeNotice`. The form's fields are `ComposeNoticeRequest` exactly, type enum
  and target shape from the contract. Submit → `composeNotice` → the URL moves to
  the new notice's detail. Refusals map to states by Problem `type`, never by prose.
- **D3 Transitions.** revise (prefilled), submit, approve, decline (reason field —
  the contract has one, `reasonCode`, and no vocabulary for it: a finding), publish,
  correct, withdraw. Each: confirm step → act → refetch detail → render. Never
  optimistic; never a client-side state change. `stateEnteredAt` beside the state as
  locale-aware relative time, with the response's own `meta.generatedAt` as "now" so
  fixture mode is deterministic.
- **D4 Bilingual authoring.** Both languages always shown whatever the UI locale.
  "This language is missing" is its own state: pre-act from the form's own emptiness,
  post-act from the publish refusal's Problem type. Bite: collapse the two into one
  generic panel → reds.
- **D5 Four states × two locales**, each failing for its own reason, for: the compose
  form; each transition confirm; the detail action bar (empty vs populated
  `permittedActions`); the refusal panel; the bilingual-missing state.
- **D6 R3 outstanding acknowledgements** bound to `notices/outstanding.json`, now
  present. Fields the contract carries; the recipient identity column is a NAMED
  deferral to the PO's G7 ruling, not a placeholder column.
- **D7 Data-claim register complete:** every rendered field → fixture path; every
  control → operationId. No register, no merge.

**Out of scope:** grant admin; cases; resident app; attachment upload; templates; any
change to contracts, fixtures, or contract-generated files. A missing or wrong fixture
is a G-finding and a HARD STOP on that item.

Cites `UI-045` (step 1 ruling, §G), `GEN-047` and its Amendment 1, `ADR-019`,
`ADR-008`, and the 2026-09-02 admin-console direction brief.
