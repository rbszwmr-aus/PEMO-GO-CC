# GEN-044 — C2: cases intake, triage and the open-case act (cut C)

**ACTS ONLY.** No routes, no handling, no read surface — those are C3 and the
routes PID.

- **D1** five acts: `submitByResident`, `submitByStaff`, `triageTicket`,
  `linkRelatedTicket`, and the composition-only `openCaseFromTicket`.
- **D2** the **reportable set** — a resident may report about their own unit and
  the Locations that SERVE it, and nothing else. A new authority shape: not a
  grant, but where they live.
- **D3** `helpdesk.intake_policy`, **fail-closed**: no row means anonymous is not
  permitted.
- **D4** opening a Case is **ATOMIC** — the Case row and the Ticket's link are one
  transaction, because a Case whose Ticket does not point back is an orphan
  nothing can reach.
- **D6** staff acts are audited with the witness; a resident's submit writes **no
  audit row**, per the ADR-014 amendment.
- **D7** the display reference, generated in the submit transaction.
