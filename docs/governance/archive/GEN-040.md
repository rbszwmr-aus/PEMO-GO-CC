# GEN-040 — N2: the notices write surface (cut B)

**ACTS ONLY.** No routes and no HTTP shape — wiring these to `management-web` is
the next cut-B PID, exactly as `W2` built the grant acts before `W3` wired them.
No read surface, no contract change.

- **D1** eight acts, every transition an assertion: compose, revise, submit,
  approve, decline, publish, correct, withdraw. Expiry stays computed.
- **D2** ADR-018's typed scope **generalised to `RoleScope<R>`** — the brand
  carries the role, so a staff scope cannot be passed where an approver scope is
  required, and ADR-017's covering conjunction applies to ordinary roles.
- **D3** `notices.approval_policy`, **fail-closed**: no row means approval is
  required.
- **D4** publish resolves and snapshots the audience as at the act, refusing a
  half-translated notice, an inverted schedule, and an audience of nobody.
- **D5** `notice.published` and `notice.withdrawn` on the outbox, in the same
  transaction. Nothing here delivers anything.
- **D6** a reason vocabulary and a declared idempotency stance per act.
- **D7** a per-notice advisory lock around every transition.
