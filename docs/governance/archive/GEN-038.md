# GEN-038 — C1: helpdesk and service_case schemas (cut A)

**ADR-020 origination only.** All write surfaces and routes are cut C; N2/N3 are
cut B. **No contract change.**

- **D1/D2** two schemas, no foreign key between them or to anything else; the
  Ticket as an ADR-013 assertion chain with computed state and no UPDATE path.
- **D3/D4** intake and triage facts on the assertion — nullable reporter, declared
  channel, a declared queue identifier rather than an invented team entity, and a
  per-organization display reference distinct from the stable id.
- **D5** `ticket_message` with immutable per-message visibility — the only column
  a resident projection may filter on.
- **D6** the Case, whose states include `pending_verification`: an external
  completion may propose, never close.
- **D7** `case_note` with **no visibility column at all**, so a resident-visible
  Case note is a migration rather than a value.
- **D8** `case_external_link`, plus a forbidden-terms denylist derived by diffing
  the interest list, scanning column **types** as well as names.
- **D9** the Ticket owns the link; `service_case` reaches its Tickets only through
  a declared `helpdesk` port.
- **D10** vocabularies declared once and projected; seeds carry the two-Ticket Case
  and the visibility split.
