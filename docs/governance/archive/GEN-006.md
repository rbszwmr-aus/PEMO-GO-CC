# GEN-006 — Authority-spine migration: close Gaps 1 and 2

## What to build

One migration, both gaps, because Gap 2 re-keys the exact constraint Gap 1 modifies; splitting them means two migrations fighting over one key. `0112` is **immutable** — do not edit it; alter the constraint in a new migration.

**Gap 1 — the exclusion key must include `role`.** `ADR-010` `D4` requires `role` to join `(…, node_kind, node_id, tstzrange)`. As built (`0112:59-64`) two grants differing only by role collide. Read `ADR-010` `D4` for the decided shape; do not take the column list from prose.

**Gap 2 — a grant is held by a group, never by a principal directly** (`ADR-012` `D2`/`D3`). The built table keys on `principal_id`. Re-key to the group. Each existing `principal_id` grant becomes a **group-of-one** — `ADR-012` is explicit that a group of one is the normal way to give one person one permission, not a workaround, so this is the decided mapping, not an invention. A person's rights are the union over their group memberships; the model stays monotonic — no deny, no exclusion.

## Done when

Proven by making it fail, not by argument.

1. **Two grants differing only by role no longer collide.** Inject the colliding pair; show it collides pre-fix and both persist post-fix. *(Gap 1 red)*
2. **A grant cannot be held by a principal directly.** Attempt a principal-held grant and show the schema rejects it. *(Gap 2 red)*
3. **A group-of-one grant gives exactly one person one permission at one node.** *(Gap 2 positive)*
4. **Re-run `modules/access`'s differential test.** `DOC-002`'s zero-disagreement green proved the **filtering**, not the **key shape** — the decisions moved after that test was written. So the re-run must (a) still pass on filtering, and (b) gain the role-collision case from (1), which would have passed under the old key and must fail-then-pass across the fix. A green that never had that case is the inherited green that is not all earned.
